When your app has customer accounts, roles, APIs, or private data, a focused manual review helps you check the boundaries that matter before a launch or major release. I test the agreed workflows, show you what I found, and help your engineers fix it.

Ask about a security review

What the review covers

The exact scope depends on your product. We choose the most important customer journeys and permission boundaries together, then write them down before testing starts.

Accounts and sessions

Login, password reset, session handling, privilege changes, and paths where a user may become another user or an administrator.

Customer and tenant isolation

Whether one account or organization can read or change another's records, files, exports, or API objects.

APIs and business workflows

Server-side authorization, hidden fields, out-of-order steps, and actions that should require a stronger check.

AI features, when present

Agent tool permissions, prompt injection paths, retrieval boundaries, and whether model-controlled input can trigger privileged actions.

What you receive

  1. Written scope and authorization: the application, roles, accounts, testing window, and any prohibited actions.
  2. Manual testing: focused on the workflows and trust boundaries selected for your review.
  3. A prioritized report: reproducible evidence, impact, root cause, and fixes an engineer can act on.
  4. One retest: after you make the agreed fixes, I check whether the original issue remains.

A scoped review provides evidence about what was tested. It does not certify an application as free of vulnerabilities or replace a broader assessment when your customer or auditor requires one.

Questions small teams ask

When should we do this?

Before a public launch, after adding major roles or tenant features, or before a customer relies on a new AI workflow. If you have an existing product, we can start with its highest-risk paths.

Do you need our source code?

No. Testing can begin with agreed test accounts and a running app. Source review can be added when it would help answer a specific question.

How much does it cost?

The current focused Launch Check starts at $750. Broader reviews depend on the roles, tenants, APIs, and workflows in scope. See current packages; you receive a fixed quote before testing.

Can a scanner do this?

Automated tools help find common technical issues. This review includes manual checks of your users, data, and workflows, where the intended permissions have to be understood to test them.

Tell me what your app must keep separate.

Send the product URL, the types of users you have, and the one boundary you most want checked. I'll respond with a proposed scope. Testing starts only after written authorization.
