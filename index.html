The finding, resolution, and credit +

The trust boundary

An unauthenticated peer reaching an exposed actor-pool port could request configuration and topology information or stop a pool. Application login did not protect this separate internal transport.

The resolution

Xoscar 0.12.0 introduced opt-in actor-transport authentication with XOSCAR_AUTH_KEY. A strong, nonempty key must be configured on communicating processes, and actor ports must be restricted to intended peers. An unset or empty key retains unauthenticated behavior; upgrading alone does not enable the protection.

My contribution

The advisory combines multiple reports. Its critical rating and title concern a separate deserialization issue. My credited contribution is the related unauthenticated control-plane exposure.

Is this right for my stage?

This is for startups bringing AI agents, LLM features or SaaS products to customers. A useful starting point is a working application with test accounts, especially before a launch, a customer review or a significant change to permissions.

What does a security review cost?

The Security Review is $2,500 for an agreed scope. Break + Fix is $3,500–$5,000, and Monthly Testing starts at $500 per month for a reserved testing block. The first three founding-client Security Reviews are $1,250; ask about availability. We agree on scope and price before testing starts.

How long will it take, and what do you need?

Timing depends on your application, the roles and workflows in scope, and availability. We agree on the testing window before starting. Prepare a product overview, your priorities and any customer deadline; test accounts and access are arranged after scope and authorization are agreed.

What is tested beyond prompt injection?

Agent tool permissions, retrieval of private data, API authorization, tenant isolation and business logic. The question is whether an input can cross a real access or data boundary. The exact checks depend on your product and the written scope.

Can I share the results with customers?

You receive a plain-language summary letter as well as the technical report. Share your customer’s requirements before we agree the scope so we can check fit. Each customer decides what evidence they accept; this review is not a compliance certification or a guarantee that the application is free of vulnerabilities.

Do you help fix findings?

Every confirmed finding includes a recommended fix, and a one-time review includes one retest of the agreed fixes. Choose Break + Fix when you want hands-on remediation work with your engineers. Monthly Testing can cover changes and fix verification in an agreed testing block.

What happens on the intro call?

We discuss what you’re building, the data and actions it handles, your priorities and timing. If there’s a fit, the next step is a written scope and quote. You do not need to bring credentials, customer data or a finished security questionnaire to the call.
